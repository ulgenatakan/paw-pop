Paw Pop Support

Paw Pop: Cat Match 3 Puzzle

Need help? Email us at dev@horusstudios.com and we'll get back to you. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

How do I restore my purchases?

On the map, tap the gear icon, then Restore Purchases. You can also find it at the bottom of the Shop. Lucky Paw is restored automatically on the same Apple Account.

How do lives work?

You have 5 lives (7 with Lucky Paw). Starting a level uses a life and winning gives it back. A new life arrives every 25 minutes, or you can refill them with coins.

I'm stuck on a level

Try a pre-game booster like Zoomies or Bubble Cat, or use Paw Smash on the cat that's in the way. Matching near crates and on top of ice clears them faster.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your device. It is kept when you update the app, but deleting the app also deletes your progress.

Do I need the internet?

No. Paw Pop plays fully offline. You only need a connection to make or restore purchases.
