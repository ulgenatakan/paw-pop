Privacy Policy

Paw Pop: Cat Match 3 Puzzle · Effective October 4, 2026

Paw Pop is made by Horus Teknoloji ve Dijital Hizmetler A.Ş. ("we"). We built Paw Pop to be a game you can enjoy without giving up any personal information.

We don't collect your data

Paw Pop does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, lives and settings) is saved only on your device. We never receive it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, payment details or Apple Account information. The app only receives the confirmation it needs to deliver the item you bought.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Paw Pop schedules reminders on your device (for example, when your lives are full). They are created locally; nothing is sent to us. You can turn them off at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

Children

Because Paw Pop collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Questions about privacy? Email us at dev@horusstudios.com.
